Functions by post

Functions vary by service, so each office type has its own function list. A post holds functions in one of two ways:

  • ALLOTTED The Establishment branch allots functions to the officer (ADC, AC), effective-dated, on the allotment grid.
  • ALL_OFFICE_FUNCTIONS The officer holds every function of the office for as long as they are posted (e.g. the Circle Officer). No allotment is entered, and a successor inherits them on transfer.

Maintained in MDM: function codes, office-type functions, designation.holdsAllFunctions and designation functions. The HR Utility copies them and refreshes when MDM changes. Only the demo bundles for ADCs and ACs outside Nagaon are local (data/config/demo_function_bundles.csv).
