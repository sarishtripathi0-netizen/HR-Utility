Who sits in which chair, holding which functions?

This PoC answers the Rule Engine's question: in office O, which chairs of post P are held at time t by an officer holding function F, are they available, and if none is, who is the Link Officer under rule 3(1)?

  • Seat lookup: ask that question as a form.
  • Office roster: every chair in an office, its holder, charge, availability and functions.
  • Function allotment (Establishment branch): officers × functions, effective-dated.
  • Posting & relieving: join, relieve with successor, declare vacant.
  • Leave entry: leave, tour, training, deputation.
  • Post history: who held a chair between two dates, with working days.
  • API docs · /openapi.json

Officers marked DEMO are synthetic. Office IDs are provisional until the MDM office seed arrives. See data/load_report.md for every data judgement.
